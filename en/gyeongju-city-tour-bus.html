Gyeongju City Tour
Tours, prices, night tour and pickup points

The Gyeongju City Tour is a guided bus tour run by Gyeongju City Tour (Cheonma Tourism). It is not hop-on hop-off: each tour visits the sights in a fixed order once a day, with pickups at Gyeongju Station, the bus terminal, Hwangnidan-gil and Bomun Lake hotels. Commentary is in Korean only.

02 · ROUTE

Gyeongju City Tour: Route and stops

The Gyeongju City Tour runs 2 routes: World Heritage Tour, East Coast Tour.

World Heritage Tour · Bulguksa · Seokguram · Cheonmachong

7–8 hours · daily, once a day · pickup Gyeongju Station 10:30 · bus terminal 10:10 · Hwangnidan-gil 10:05

  1. Tomb of King Muyeol
  2. Cheonmachong Tomb
  3. Bunhwangsa Temple
  4. Seokguram Grotto
  5. Bulguksa Temple

Admission (KRW 5,000 adult, 3,000 secondary students, 1,500 elementary) is paid separately and lunch is on your own. At the end you can get off at any of the pickup points.

East Coast Tour · Tomb of King Munmu · Golgulsa

7–8 hours · Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun · pickup Gyeongju Station 09:10 · bus terminal 09:20

  1. Gyeongju Traditional Silk Exhibition Hall
  2. Underwater Tomb of King Munmu
  3. Golgulsa Temple

Main stops as listed on the official course page. No admission fees; lunch is on your own.

03 · NIGHT TOUR

Gyeongju City Tour: Night tour

The night tour visits Gyeongju's lit-up heritage sites with a guide, walking from Cheomseongdae Observatory to Woljeonggyo Bridge. Dinner is not included, so eat before you board.

  • Departs Daily 18:30–22:00
  • From The pickup point you booked
  • Duration about 3 h 30 min
  • Price KRW 20,000 adult · KRW 18,000 youth and child (admission included)

06 · HOW TO RIDE

Gyeongju City Tour: How to ride

  1. Be at your pickup point on time: for the World Heritage Tour, Gyeongju Station 10:30, the bus terminal 10:10, Hwangnidan-gil (Bonghwangdae parking lot) 10:05.
  2. Give your booking name to the guide. Seats are unreserved.
  3. Commentary is in Korean only.
  4. At the end you can get off at any pickup point; book your train or bus home for after 18:00.

07 · FAQ

Gyeongju City Tour: FAQ

Is the Gyeongju City Tour hop-on hop-off?

No. It is a once-a-day guided tour that visits the sights in a fixed order.

Is there English commentary?

No. Commentary is in Korean only, although the booking system has an English option and accepts foreign cards.

How much is the Gyeongju City Tour?

The World Heritage and East Coast tours are KRW 25,000 for adults; the night tour is KRW 20,000 including admission.

Can I join from Gyeongju Station?

Yes. Pickups include Gyeongju Station, the bus terminal, Hwangnidan-gil and the main Bomun Lake hotels; times differ by tour.

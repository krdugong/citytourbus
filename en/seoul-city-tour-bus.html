Seoul City Tour Bus
Routes, prices, night tour and where to board

The Seoul City Tour Bus (run by Tiger Bus) has left from Gwanghwamun since 2000. By day it loops past Gyeongbokgung Palace, Myeong-dong and N Seoul Tower on a hop-on hop-off course; at night a 90-minute tour crosses the Han River bridges and stops at Namsan.

The ticket is valid for that day only, and you can get on and off at the 15 stops as often as you like. The last bus leaves Gwanghwamun at 16:50 and returns at 18:20, so check the time before getting off at N Seoul Tower or Dongdaemun.

03 · NIGHT TOUR

Seoul City Tour Bus: Night tour

The night course leaves Gwanghwamun, crosses Mapo, Hangang, Dongjak, Banpo, Seongsu and Hannam bridges, stops at N Seoul Tower for about 25 minutes and ends at Cheonggye Plaza via Sungnyemun. N Seoul Tower is its only stop.

  • Departs 19:00 once a day (19:30 from 1 May to 31 Aug)
  • From Gwanghwamun ticket office (boarding from 40 min before)
  • Duration about 90 min (about 25 min stop at N Seoul Tower)
  • Price Adult from KRW 25,000 · child from KRW 16,000

06 · HOW TO RIDE

Seoul City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. Walk 100 m from Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5) exit 6, or about 300 m from City Hall Station (Lines 1 and 2) exit 3, to the Gwanghwamun ticket office.
  2. Give the name on your booking at the ticket office and collect your boarding pass.
  3. Show the pass to the driver when you board. Seats are first come, first served.
  4. The audio guide is available in 12 languages; ask at the ticket office if you need earphones.
  5. Get off at any of the 15 stops and catch the next bus, which comes every 25 minutes.

07 · FAQ

Seoul City Tour Bus: FAQ

Can I ride the Seoul City Tour Bus without booking?

Yes. Same-day tickets cannot be bought online; buy them at the Gwanghwamun ticket office. For the night course it is worth calling 02-777-6090 to check seats first.

Where do I board the Seoul City Tour Bus?

At the Gwanghwamun ticket office, 100 m from Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5) exit 6 and about 300 m from City Hall Station exit 3.

Does it run on Mondays?

No, it is closed every Monday. If Monday is a public holiday it runs and closes on Tuesday instead.

Is there an English audio guide?

Yes. The day course has an audio guide in 12 languages, including English.

Can I choose an open-top double-decker?

No. The bus type (open-top double-decker, glass-top double-decker or single-deck) is assigned on the day.

Daegu City Tour Bus
City Circle route, timetable and prices

The Daegu City Tour is a hop-on hop-off loop from Dongdaegu Station past Kim Kwang-seok Street, Dongseong-ro, the Modern Culture Alley, Seomun Market, E-World, Apsan, Suseong Lake and the Daegu Art Museum. Buy your ticket from the driver and ride all day. Foreign visitors get 20% off.

04 · BOOKING

Daegu City Tour Bus: How to book

  1. No booking needed: pay the driver on board by cash, card or transport card.
  2. Show your passport for the 20% foreigner discount; every Wednesday there is a 30% discount event.
  3. Seats are first come, first served and standing is not allowed; if the bus is full, take the next one an hour later.
Official Daegu City Tour site →

05 · HOW TO RIDE

Daegu City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. Board at the stop in front of Dongdaegu Station plaza, or at any other stop.
  2. Pay as you board and keep your ticket; a lost ticket cannot be replaced.
  3. Get off anywhere and catch the next bus an hour later.
  4. The last bus leaves Dongdaegu Station at 15:30 and returns around 17:50.

06 · FAQ

Daegu City Tour Bus: FAQ

Does the Daegu City Tour run every day?

Tuesday to Sunday. It is closed every Monday and on Lunar New Year and Chuseok days.

How much is the Daegu City Tour?

KRW 10,000 for adults, or KRW 8,000 with the 20% discount for foreign visitors.

Do I need to book?

No. Buy your ticket from the driver when you board.

Where does it start?

At Dongdaegu Station, where KTX and SRT trains arrive; you can also board at any other stop.

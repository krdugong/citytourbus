Yeosu City Tour Bus
Double-decker, night and Hyangiram courses

Yeosu's "Romantic Bus" city tours, run by Yeosu City, all leave from Yeosu Expo Station. The double-decker day loop circles Odongdo, Yi Sun-sin Square and Dolsan Park entrance in 35 minutes and you pay as you board; in the evening a double-decker night course and a night view course show the famous Yeosu night sea. Course 1 (Hyangiram) and Course 2 (island bridges) are full-day guided tours.

02 · ROUTE

Yeosu City Tour Bus: Route and stops

The Yeosu City Tour Bus runs 4 routes: Double-decker day loop, Course 1 (Hyangiram), Course 2 (island bridges), Night view course.

Double-decker day loop · Odongdo · Yi Sun-sin Square · Dolsan Park

35 min per loop · hourly (6 a day, no 13:30 run) · 10:30–16:30 from Yeosu Expo Station

  1. Yeosu Expo Station (start)
  2. Aqua Planet
  3. Cable car parking tower
  4. Odongdo Island (music fountain)
  5. Sono Calm Yeosu
  6. Hamel Exhibition Hall
  7. Yi Sun-sin Square
  8. Seafood market
  9. Dolsan Park entrance

Pay by cash or card when you board; one ticket covers up to six rides. Closed the first and third Monday of each month. The wristband ticket gives discounts at Aqua Planet, the sea cable car and more.

Course 1 (Hyangiram) · Odongdo · Jinnamgwan · Hyangiram

6 h 50 min · daily (closed 2nd Monday) · 10:30 from Yeosu Expo Station → back 17:20

  1. Odongdo Island
  2. Jinnamgwan Hall (lunch)
  3. Marine Science Museum
  4. Hyangiram Hermitage
  5. Fish market

The Marine Science Museum (KRW 3,000) is closed on Mondays and skipped that day.

Course 2 (island bridges) · Jangdo · Soho Dongdong Bridge · Nangdo

about 7 h · closed Sundays and 4th Monday · 10:30 from Yeosu Expo Station → back 17:30

  1. Jangdo Island (Ungcheon)
  2. Soho Dongdong Bridge (walk and lunch)
  3. Jobaldo Observation Park
  4. Nangdo Yeosan Village
  5. Palyeong Bridge / Jeokgeum Park

Crosses the island bridges between Yeosu and Goheung; the current route started on 1 September 2026.

Night view course · Odongdo · industrial complex · Namsan Park

2 h · daily · 19:30 from Yeosu Expo Station (19:00 Nov–Feb)

  1. Odongdo Island (music fountain)
  2. Yeosu National Industrial Complex night view
  3. Namsan Park

New route from 1 September 2026. The Odongdo fountain runs March–October; when it is off, the tour goes to Hamel Lighthouse instead.

03 · NIGHT TOUR

Yeosu City Tour Bus: Night tour

The double-decker night course stops for 50 minutes at Dolsan Park overlooking Yeosu's night sea, walks Soho Dongdong Bridge for 30 minutes, then passes Hidden Bay and Yi Sun-sin Square to Jongpo Ocean Park (you can get off) and returns around 22:00. It does not run on the first and third Mondays, or on Fridays and Saturdays when the "Busker" bus runs.

  • Departs 19:30 (19:00 November–February), once a day
  • From Yeosu Expo Station
  • Duration 2 h 30 min (50 min at Dolsan Park, 30 min at Soho Dongdong Bridge)
  • Price KRW 10,000 (KRW 5,000 concession)

05 · BOOKING

Yeosu City Tour Bus: How to book

  1. The double-decker day loop needs no booking: pay by cash or card as you board.
  2. Book the night, night view and day courses online first (Korean site, button below); spare seats are sold on site.
  3. Questions: Dongseo Tour 061-692-0900 (double-decker), Boram Express Tour 061-902-0212 (Courses 1 and 2, night view).

06 · HOW TO RIDE

Yeosu City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. All courses leave from Yeosu Expo Station.
  2. The double-decker day loop comes hourly from 10:30 (no 13:30 run) and you can board at any stop.
  3. Evening courses leave at 19:30 (19:00 November–February); arrive 10 minutes early.
  4. Arrange your own travel insurance.

07 · FAQ

Yeosu City Tour Bus: FAQ

Where do I board the Yeosu city tour bus?

All courses leave from Yeosu Expo Station; the double-decker day loop can also be boarded at Odongdo, Yi Sun-sin Square and its other stops.

How much is the Yeosu double-decker bus?

The day loop is KRW 5,000 for up to six rides; the night course is KRW 10,000.

Do I need to book?

Not for the day loop. The night and day courses are booked online first, with spare seats sold on site.

Is there a tour to Hyangiram?

Yes, Course 1 leaves Yeosu Expo Station at 10:30 and visits Odongdo, Jinnamgwan and Hyangiram, returning at 17:20.

Incheon City Tour Bus
Retro and Sea Line timetables and prices

The Incheon City Tour starts at the Incheon Tourist Information Center in Songdo Central Park. The Retro Line loops through Chinatown, Sinpo International Market and Incheon Art Platform; the double-decker Sea Line visits Incheon Airport Terminal 2, Eurwangni Beach and Paradise City. A combined ticket covers both. It runs Wednesday to Sunday.

02 · ROUTE

Incheon City Tour Bus: Route and stops

The Incheon City Tour Bus runs 2 routes: Retro Line, Sea Line.

Retro Line · Chinatown · Sinpo Market · open-port area

about 1 h 30 min per loop · hourly (no 12:30 run) · 10:30–16:30 from Songdo (6 a day)

  1. Incheon Tourist Information Center (Songdo Central Park)
  2. Sinpo International Market (Dapdong Cathedral)
  3. Hwapyeong-dong Naengmyeon Street (Jayu Park)
  4. Incheon Station (Chinatown)
  5. Sangsang Platform
  6. Incheon Art Platform (Harbor Park Hotel)
  7. Songdo Convensia
  8. Hyundai Premium Outlet Songdo

Chinatown, Jayu Park and the open-port streets are an easy walk from the Incheon Station stop. Runs as a minibus.

Sea Line · Incheon Airport · Eurwangni · Paradise City

about 2 h 35 min per loop · hourly (7 a day) · 10:00–16:00 from Songdo

  1. Incheon Tourist Information Center (Songdo Central Park)
  2. Songdo Convensia
  3. Incheon Airport Terminal 2
  4. INSPIRE Entertainment Resort
  5. Eurwangni Beach
  6. Muuido entrance
  7. Paradise City (art docent tour)
  8. G-Tower (IFEZ Promotion Center)

There is a stop at Incheon Airport Terminal 2 (near exit 6, ground floor). Double-decker; Eurwangni Beach and the Muuido entrance have the best sea views.

05 · HOW TO RIDE

Incheon City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. Board at the Incheon Tourist Information Center outside exit 3 of Central Park Station (Incheon Line 1).
  2. If you booked, show ID (a passport for visitors) at the center before boarding.
  3. Scan the QR code of your ticket or mobile voucher when boarding; you can hop on and off that line all day.
  4. Seats are first come, first served and standing is not allowed.

06 · FAQ

Incheon City Tour Bus: FAQ

Which days does the Incheon City Tour run?

Wednesday to Sunday. It is closed every Monday and Tuesday and on Lunar New Year and Chuseok days.

What is the difference between the Sea Line and the Retro Line?

The Retro Line is a 1 h 30 min minibus loop through Chinatown and old Incheon; the Sea Line is a 2 h 35 min double-decker loop to Incheon Airport T2, Eurwangni Beach and Paradise City.

Can I board at Incheon Airport?

Yes, the Sea Line stops at Terminal 2, near exit 6 on the ground floor.

Is the combined ticket worth it?

If you ride both lines, yes: KRW 16,000 instead of KRW 23,000 for two separate adult tickets.

FAQ

Is there a hop-on hop-off bus in South Korea?

Yes. Seoul (the Seoul City Tour Bus, also called the Tiger Bus), Busan and Jeju run hop-on hop-off loops with day passes, Gyeongju, Incheon, Daegu, Yeosu, Andong and Paju run their own city tour buses too.

Is there a hop-on hop-off bus in Seoul?

Yes. The Seoul City Tour Bus (Tiger Bus) leaves Gwanghwamun every 25 minutes from 09:20 to 16:50 on a 15-stop loop past Gyeongbokgung Palace, Myeong-dong, N Seoul Tower and DDP. It is closed on Mondays and also runs a 90-minute Han River night tour.

Is there a hop-on hop-off bus in Busan?

Yes. The Busan City Tour Bus runs Red (Haeundae), Green (Taejongdae) and Orange (Dadaepo) lines from Busan Station on one KRW 20,000 day pass, Wednesday to Sunday.

Is there a hop-on hop-off bus in Jeju?

Yes. The Jeju City Tour Bus loops 22 stops around Jeju City, including Jeju Airport stop 3, on a KRW 12,000 day pass you buy when you board. It is closed on Mondays.

Do Korean city tour buses run on Mondays?

Mostly not. Seoul and Jeju close every Monday, and Busan closes on Mondays and Tuesdays. Check each city page before you plan.

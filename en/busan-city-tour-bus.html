Busan City Tour Bus
Hop-on hop-off lines, route map, timetable and prices

The Busan City Tour Bus is a loop bus from Busan Station. One day pass covers three lines: the Red Line to Gwangalli and Haeundae, the Green Line to Huinnyeoul Culture Village and Taejongdae, and the Orange Line to Gamcheon Culture Village and Dadaepo. In the evening a double-decker night tour crosses Gwangan Bridge. It runs Wednesday to Sunday.

02 · ROUTE

Busan City Tour Bus: Route and stops

The Busan City Tour Bus runs 3 routes: Red Line (Busan Station ↔ Haeundae), Green Line (Busan Station ↔ Taejongdae), Orange Line (Busan Station ↔ Dadaepo).

Red Line (Busan Station ↔ Haeundae) · Haeundae · Gwangalli · Marine City

about 2 h 10 min per loop · every 50 min (9 a day) · 09:45–16:35 from Busan Station

  1. Busan Station (start)
  2. UN Memorial Cemetery
  3. Busan Museum
  4. Yonghoman Cruise Terminal
  5. Gwangalli Beach
  6. Marine City (Suyeong Bay Yacht Stadium)
  7. Dongbaekseom Island
  8. Haeundae Beach
  9. Centum City (Busan Cinema Center)
  10. Busan Museum of Art / BEXCO
  11. Peace Park
  12. Gwangbok-ro

Crosses Busan Harbor Bridge and Gwangan Bridge. The 16:35 run skips the UN Memorial Cemetery and Busan Museum, and transfers are hard on the last three runs. Single- or double-deck buses are assigned on the day.

Green Line (Busan Station ↔ Taejongdae) · Yeongdo · Taejongdae · Oryukdo

about 2 h 10 min per loop · every 50 min (9 a day) · 09:30–16:20 from Busan Station

  1. Busan Station (start)
  2. Yeongdo Bridge (Yurari Plaza)
  3. Huinnyeoul Culture Village
  4. Sky Observatory
  5. Taejongdae
  6. National Maritime Museum
  7. ARTE Museum Busan
  8. Oryukdo Skywalk
  9. Yonghoman Cruise Terminal
  10. Peace Park
  11. Songdo Beach (Cloud Trail)
  12. Jagalchi / BIFF Square (Yongdusan Park)

Change to the Red Line at Yonghoman Cruise Terminal or Peace Park. On Saturdays the sixth run skips Yeongdo Bridge during the bridge-lifting show.

Orange Line (Busan Station ↔ Dadaepo) · Gamcheon Culture Village · Dadaepo

about 2 h 10 min per loop · every 80 min (6 a day) · 09:20–16:00 from Busan Station

  1. Busan Station (start)
  2. Songdo Beach (Songdo Cloud Trail)
  3. Amnam Park (Yonggung Cloud Bridge)
  4. Gamcheon Culture Village
  5. Dadaepo Beach (Molundae)
  6. Amisan Observatory
  7. Bunecia Jangnim Port
  8. Seokdang Museum
  9. Gukje Market (Bosu-dong Book Street)
  10. Yongdusan Park

Runs single-deck buses only because of steep hill roads. Change to the Green Line at the Green Line stop across from Songdo Beach.

03 · NIGHT TOUR

Busan City Tour Bus: Night tour

The night bridge tour crosses Busan Bridge and Busan Harbor Bridge, stops 10 minutes at Gwangalli Beach, passes Haeundae and Marine City, crosses Gwangan Bridge and stops again at Songdo Cloud Trail before returning to Busan Station. It is a double-decker, booked online in advance, and may be cancelled if too few people book.

  • Departs 19:30 (19:00 November–March)
  • From Busan Station, opposite Starbucks at Ramada Encore
  • Duration about 2 h 10 min (10-min photo stops at Gwangalli and Songdo Cloud Trail)
  • Price KRW 25,000 adult · KRW 15,000 child

06 · HOW TO RIDE

Busan City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. Leave KTX Busan Station by exit 1 (or subway Busan Station exit 6) and go to the stop opposite Starbucks on the ground floor of Ramada Encore.
  2. Show your ticket and board in arrival order.
  3. One day pass covers the Red, Green and Orange Lines; change lines at the transfer stops (Yonghoman Cruise Terminal, Peace Park, Songdo Beach).
  4. Late runs make transfers hard, so start in the morning if you want to ride several lines.

Busan City Tour Bus: Quick answers

Short answers to the questions people search for most, based on the official information on this page.

Busan hop-on hop-off bus route map

All three lines start and end at Busan Station. The Red Line runs to Gwangalli, Haeundae and Centum City, the Green Line to Huinnyeoul Culture Village and Taejongdae, and the Orange Line to Gamcheon Culture Village and Dadaepo. The maps on this page number every stop in order. See the route and map →

Busan City Tour Bus timetable

The Red Line leaves Busan Station from 09:45 to 16:35 and the Green Line from 09:30 to 16:20, both every 50 minutes (9 a day); the Orange Line leaves from 09:20 to 16:00 every 80 minutes (6 a day). Each loop takes about 2 hours 10 minutes, and buses run Wednesday to Sunday. See the route and map →

Busan City Tour Bus ticket price

One day pass covers the Red, Green and Orange lines: KRW 20,000 for adults and KRW 10,000 for children, or KRW 19,000 and KRW 9,500 when bought online in advance. The night bridge tour is KRW 25,000 for adults and KRW 15,000 for children. See prices →

Busan City Tour Bus Red Line vs Green Line

Take the Red Line for beaches and the modern city: Gwangalli, Haeundae, Dongbaekseom and Centum City. Take the Green Line for the sea cliffs and Yeongdo: Huinnyeoul Culture Village, Taejongdae, Oryukdo Skywalk and Songdo. You can change lines at Yonghoman Cruise Terminal or Peace Park on the same pass. See the route and map →

Busan City Tour Bus and the Visit Busan Pass

Visit Busan Pass sellers list the Busan City Tour Bus among the pass benefits; you exchange the pass for a bus ticket at Busan Station before you ride. Check what your pass type includes before relying on it. See how to book →

Busan night bus tour (Gwangan Bridge)

The night bridge tour is a double-decker that leaves Busan Station at 19:30 (19:00 from November to March), crosses Busan Harbor Bridge and Gwangan Bridge, and stops 10 minutes at Gwangalli Beach and Songdo Cloud Trail. It must be booked online in advance. See the night tour →

Where to board the Busan City Tour Bus

Leave KTX Busan Station by exit 1 (or subway Busan Station exit 6) and go to the stop opposite Starbucks on the ground floor of the Ramada Encore hotel. You can also board at any stop on the lines. See where to board →

Is the Busan City Tour Bus worth it?

It is worth it if you want Haeundae, Taejongdae or Gamcheon in a day without bus transfers, as the day pass covers all three lines. Start in the morning, because later runs make line changes hard, and remember it does not run on Mondays and Tuesdays. See the summary →

08 · FAQ

Busan City Tour Bus: FAQ

Is there a hop-on hop-off bus in Busan?

Yes. The Busan City Tour Bus is Busan's hop-on hop-off bus: one KRW 20,000 day pass covers the Red, Green and Orange lines from Busan Station, Wednesday to Sunday.

Does the Busan City Tour Bus run every day?

No. It runs Wednesday to Sunday and is closed Monday and Tuesday.

Do I need to book the Busan City Tour Bus?

Not for the loop lines: board at the stop on a first-come basis. Only the night tour needs advance booking.

Does one ticket cover all lines?

Yes. One day pass covers the Red, Green and Orange loop lines. The night tour and themed routes are sold separately.

Where does the Busan City Tour Bus leave from?

Busan Station, opposite Starbucks on the ground floor of the Ramada Encore hotel (KTX Busan Station exit 1).

Is it a double-decker?

The Red and Green Lines use single- or double-deck buses assigned on the day; the Orange Line is single-deck only; the night tour is a double-decker.

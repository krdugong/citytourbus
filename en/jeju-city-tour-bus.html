Jeju City Tour Bus
Loop route, prices and timetable

The Jeju City Tour Bus is Jeju's hop-on hop-off bus. It loops around Jeju City past the ferry terminals, Dongmun Market, Yongduam Rock, Jeju International Airport, the rainbow coastal road at Dodubong, Iho Tewoo Beach, the five-day market, Dream Tower and the bus terminal. A day pass lets you hop on and off at 22 stops, and you simply pay when you board. The route and timetable changed on 3 November 2026.

04 · BOOKING

Jeju City Tour Bus: How to book

  1. No booking needed: pay when you board at any stop (credit card or transport card).
  2. Choose the day pass (KRW 12,000) for several stops or the single ride (KRW 5,000) for one trip.
  3. Service may stop in typhoons or heavy rain or snow; call 064-741-8785 to check.

05 · HOW TO RIDE

Jeju City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. At Jeju Airport, board at bus stop 3 on the 1st floor; you can also board at any of the 22 stops.
  2. Pay as you board and keep your ticket; show the day pass when you reboard.
  3. Get off anywhere and catch the next bus an hour later. The last full loop leaves the first stop at 16:30.
  4. Pets can ride only in a bag or carrier.

07 · FAQ

Jeju City Tour Bus: FAQ

Is there a hop-on hop-off bus in Jeju?

Yes. The Jeju City Tour Bus is Jeju's hop-on hop-off bus: one KRW 12,000 day pass covers unlimited rides at 22 stops around Jeju City, including Jeju Airport, Dongmun Market and the coastal road.

Do I need to book the Jeju City Tour Bus?

No. Pay when you board at any stop, by credit card or transport card.

Can I board at Jeju Airport?

Yes, at bus stop 3 on the airport's 1st floor. The bus calls on the hour from 09:00 to 17:00.

What is the Jeju City Tour Bus timetable?

Nine loops a day, hourly from 08:30 to 16:30 from Jeju Port International Passenger Terminal. A loop takes about 2 hours, and the bus reaches the airport about 30 minutes after leaving the port.

What changed on 3 November 2026?

The loop grew to 22 stops: Sheraton Jeju Hotel and Oraemul Square were added, Halla Arboretum was dropped, the Nohyeong stop moved to Wonnohyeong by Dream Tower, and the first bus moved from 09:00 to 08:30.

Does it cover the whole of Jeju Island?

No. It loops around Jeju City in the north only. For Seongsan Ilchulbong, Hallasan or Seogwipo you need a day tour, a rental car or regular buses.

Does it run on Mondays?

No, it is closed every Monday.

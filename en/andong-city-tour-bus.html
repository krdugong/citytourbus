Andong City Tour
Hahoe Village course, price and pickup times

The Andong City Tour is a guided bus tour run by a company designated by Andong City. Once a day from Tuesday to Sunday it leaves KTX Andong Station for Buyongdae Cliff, Hahoe market, the Hahoe Byeolsingut mask dance, Hahoe Folk Village and Byeongsan Seowon, returning in the evening. Admission and commentary are included.

02 · ROUTE

Andong City Tour: Route and stops

The Andong City Tour runs one route: Hahoe Village course.

Hahoe Village course · Buyongdae · Hahoe Village · Byeongsan Seowon

about 7 hours · Tue–Sun, once a day · 10:50 Old Andong Station · 11:25 KTX Andong Station → back 17:50

  1. Old Andong Station (Iljik Restaurant)
  2. KTX Andong Station
  3. Buyongdae Cliff
  4. Hahoe market (lunch on your own)
  5. Hahoe Byeolsingut mask dance (13:50)
  6. Hahoe Folk Village
  7. Byeongsan Seowon
  8. KTX Andong Station / bus terminal

From Buyongdae Cliff you see the whole of Hahoe Village inside the bend of the Nakdong River. The mask dance runs at 14:00 Tue–Sun from March to December and on weekends in January and February; on other days the tour visits a soju distillery, hanji paper workshop or the provincial government complex instead.

04 · BOOKING

Andong City Tour: How to book

  1. Open the official site with the button below and book the Hahoe Village course.
  2. The tour runs with at least 5 people, so book ahead once you know your date.
  3. Foreign visitors can pay on the day; for help in English or other languages call the Gyeongbuk Tourist Information Center (054-852-6800).

05 · HOW TO RIDE

Andong City Tour: How to ride

  1. Board at Old Andong Station (in front of Iljik Restaurant) at 10:50 or in front of KTX Andong Station at 11:25.
  2. Give your name to the guide.
  3. Have lunch at Hahoe market, watch the 13:50 mask dance, then visit Hahoe Village and Byeongsan Seowon.
  4. You are back at KTX Andong Station around 17:50.

06 · FAQ

Andong City Tour: FAQ

Where does the Andong City Tour start?

At Old Andong Station at 10:50 and in front of KTX Andong Station at 11:25.

What does the price include?

Admission fees and the guide. Meals and personal expenses are extra.

Will I see the Hahoe mask dance?

Yes, the 14:00 performance (Tue–Sun March–December, weekends in January–February). On days without a show another stop replaces it.

Can foreign visitors join?

Yes. Foreign visitors can pay on the day, and the Gyeongbuk Tourist Information Center (054-852-6800) helps in other languages.

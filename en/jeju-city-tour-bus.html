Jeju City Tour Bus
Loop route, prices and timetable

The Jeju City Tour Bus loops around Jeju City: the ferry terminals, Dongmun Market, Yongduam Rock, Jeju International Airport, the rainbow coastal road at Dodubong, Iho Tewoo Beach, the five-day market, Halla Arboretum and the bus terminal. A day pass lets you hop on and off at 21 stops, and you pay when you board. From 3 November 2026 the route and timetable change.

04 · BOOKING

Jeju City Tour Bus: How to book

  1. No booking needed: pay when you board at any stop (credit card or transport card).
  2. Choose the day pass (KRW 12,000) for several stops or the single ride (KRW 5,000) for one trip.
  3. Service may stop in typhoons or heavy rain or snow; call 064-741-8784 to check.
Official Jeju City Tour site →

05 · HOW TO RIDE

Jeju City Tour Bus: How to ride

  1. At Jeju Airport, board at bus stop 3; you can also board at any of the 21 stops.
  2. Pay as you board and keep your ticket; show the day pass when you reboard.
  3. Get off anywhere and catch the next bus an hour later. The last bus leaves at 17:00.
  4. Pets can ride only in a bag or carrier.

06 · FAQ

Jeju City Tour Bus: FAQ

Do I need to book the Jeju City Tour Bus?

No. Pay when you board at any stop.

Can I board at Jeju Airport?

Yes, at bus stop 3 outside the airport; the bus passes about every hour.

Does it run on Mondays?

No, it is closed every Monday.

How often does it come?

Hourly, with the last departure at 17:00. Times change from 3 November 2026, so check the official timetable.
